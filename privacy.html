Sauvenza Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated November 19, 2026. Applies to Sauvenza version 1.0.2 and later.

Summary: Sauvenza stores your tabs, workspaces and snapshots only on your own device. Your browsing data is never sent to us or anyone else, there are no analytics or telemetry, and we never sell or share data. Sauvenza only goes online for Pro: to check a Pro key, and to send the order form if you choose to buy Pro.

1. What Sauvenza stores, and where

To save and restore your sessions, Sauvenza reads the following from Chrome and stores it locally in your browser profile (IndexedDB and chrome.storage.local):

  • Page titles and URLs of tabs in your normal (non-incognito) windows.
  • Window layout, pinned state, and tab group titles, colours and collapsed state.
  • Workspaces you save, and automatic snapshots of your session.
  • Your Sauvenza settings, a random ID for this browser, and, if you activate Pro, your Pro key and its status (plan, expiry date and when it was last checked).

This data never leaves your device unless you export it, for example by downloading a backup or copying a window as Markdown. Pro's automatic backup writes a file to Downloads/Sauvenza-Backups/ on your own disk.

2. What Sauvenza does not do

  • No account, sign-in or cloud sync.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or tracking pixels.
  • No reading or changing the content of web pages; Sauvenza has no content scripts.
  • No saving of incognito windows.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of data with anyone.

3. Network requests

Sauvenza connects to the internet in only two cases. Both go to Sauvenza's key server, a Google Apps Script web app at script.google.com. No tab, URL or browsing data is ever included.

  • Pro key checks. When you activate a Pro key, once a day while a key is saved, and when you remove the key, Sauvenza sends the key, the random ID for this browser and the extension version. This is how a key is locked to one browser at a time and how expired or revoked keys are detected. Without a Pro key, Sauvenza never makes this request.
  • The Buy Pro order form, only if you send it. It contains what you type: the plan, payment method, amount, currency and transaction ID, your email address, and optionally your name, the account you paid from and a note. The key server emails these details to us so we can confirm your payment and send your Pro key.

Requests to the key server are processed by Google under Google's privacy policy. If you choose to contact us on WhatsApp from the Buy Pro page, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that conversation. If you pay by bank transfer, wallet or USDT, you pay in your own bank, wallet or crypto app; we never see your bank login or wallet keys.

Card payments. If you pay by card, the checkout runs on Gumroad, under Gumroad's privacy policy. Your card details go only to Gumroad and its payment processors; we never see them. After the sale, Gumroad sends our key server your email address, your name if you gave one, the product you bought, the price and a sale ID, so the key server can email your Pro key automatically.

When Show site icons is on, Chrome loads each tab's favicon directly from that website, as it would in any tab list. You can turn this off in Settings to show letter icons instead.

4. Pro customer records

If you buy Pro, we keep a private record of your order: your email address, plan, key, the details from your order form or the Gumroad sale ID, and the key's activation status (the browser ID it is locked to, and when it was activated and last checked). We use this only to deliver your key, answer support requests, move or restore your key, and keep basic sales records. We do not use it for marketing and never share it. You can ask us to delete your order details at any time; deleting them also ends the Pro key they belong to.

5. Permissions

  • tabs: read tab titles and URLs to snapshot, search and restore them.
  • tabGroups: save and recreate tab groups.
  • storage and unlimitedStorage: keep settings and long snapshot histories on your device.
  • alarms: schedule snapshots, backups and the daily Pro key check.
  • downloads: write the backup and export files you request.
  • Host permissions https://script.google.com/* and https://script.googleusercontent.com/*: the key server, for Pro key checks and the Buy Pro order form only.

6. Your control over your data

  • Export: download a full JSON backup at any time from Import / Export.
  • Delete: use Settings → Danger zone to delete all snapshots or all workspaces, or delete individual items.
  • Remove your Pro key: Settings & Pro → Remove key from this browser deletes the key from this browser and frees it on the key server.
  • Uninstall: removing the extension makes Chrome delete all of Sauvenza's stored data. Files you exported stay on your disk until you delete them.

7. Children

Sauvenza is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and describe the change on this page.
